About the role
About Proliferate
Proliferate is building the operating system for modern engineering.
We are building the company in person in SoMa, San Francisco. We care a lot about intensity, craft, and working with people who want to do the best work of their careers. We work hard, and we also take health seriously: company-expensed Whoop, meaningful health and wellness budget, and top-tier insurance.

About the Role
You’d be one of our first engineering hires and build the core product. This is a high-ownership role across product, systems, and user experience.
Example work:
Build a new core workflow end to end across agent runtime, product UI, and cloud infrastructure.
Design and ship a new interface for human-agent collaboration made possible by a model capability that landed last week.
Turn a rough customer pain point into a polished product surface in days.
Re-think how engineering teams review, redirect, and trust agent work before merge.
Who We’re Looking For
We have an extremely small, flat, talent-dense team. We value urgency, taste, truth-seeking, and people who care deeply about the quality and impact of their work.
You might be a great fit if:
You've shipped products from 0 to 1
You want a role that is unusually dense in learning, ownership, and pace
You want to work with teammates who have very high standards
You care deeply about what you build and who you build it with
You want to do your life's work and enable other people to do theirs
